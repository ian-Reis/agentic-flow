# Decisions

Log of decisions by the Owner (or by the Director on their behalf). Task prompts cite these instead of reopening them. Never delete an entry; change its status and link the replacement.

Entry format:

```
### D001 - Short title            (YYYY-MM-DD, active)
Decision: what was decided.
Why: the reason or evidence.
Affects: task IDs, docs.
Supersedes: D### (omit if none)
```

Status values: `active`, `superseded by D###`, `revoked`. Mark director-made choices `(director, pending Owner confirmation)` until the Owner has seen them.
